# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-09-24

## 1. Introduction
TradeX is a simulated trading platform built for demonstration purposes. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data.

## 2. Data We Collect
- **Profile Information:** We collect your name and email address when you register.
- **Usage Data:** We log IP addresses and basic request metadata to secure the platform and prevent abuse.
- **Trading Data:** All portfolios, orders, and transactions you make on the platform are simulated and stored in our database.

## 3. How We Use Your Data
Your email is used strictly for authentication and account recovery. We do not sell your data or share it with marketing agencies.

## 4. Your Rights (Account Deletion)
You have the right to delete your account at any time. You can do this by submitting a DELETE request to /api/v1/auth/me. This action is irreversible and permanently purges your profile, simulated portfolio, and trading history from our active databases.

## 5. Contact
Because this is a demonstration project, there is no active support team.
